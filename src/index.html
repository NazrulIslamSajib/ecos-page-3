Sustainability starts with action

We believe real change starts at home. Every time you choose a product that’s better for your family and the planet, you’re part of something bigger. That’s why we build sustainability into everything we do—from how we source ingredients to how we power our facilities. And it’s why we’re committed to a climate positive clean.

  • Carbon Neutral

    Since 2013, we’ve operated as a carbon neutral company. We use 100% renewable electricity at all U.S. facilities, invest in energy efficiency, and reduce emissions across our supply chain. Each year, we offset more than 28 million pounds of CO₂.

  • Water Neutral

    We’ve balanced our water use since 2016 by reducing consumption, concentrating our formulas, and restoring over 14 million gallons of water to endangered ecosystems every year.

  • Zero Waste

    In 2015, we became TRUE Platinum Zero Waste certified. More than 99% of our manufacturing and office waste is reused, recycled, or repurposed—so it doesn’t end up in landfills or incinerators.

What does It mean to be Eco-Conscious?

Eco-conscious means thinking through every step, from sourcing to disposal. We choose ingredients based on their safety for people and the planet. We power our facilities with renewable electricity and constantly improve our processes to reduce our impact. Our certifications, including LEED Zero Carbon, LEED Zero Energy, and LEED Zero Waste, reflect a deeper commitment to doing what’s right, not just what’s required. With more than 55 years of experience, we know that big change starts small. Every refill, every load of laundry, every bottle you choose makes a difference.

How Do We Protect Biodiversity?

Biodiversity is nature’s masterpiece—an intricate web of plants, animals, fungi, microbes, and ecosystems that all work together to keep life in balance. It influences everything from the air we breathe to the food we grow, the water we drink, and even the stability of our climate. But this delicate balance is under pressure. Pollution, habitat destruction, and chemical overuse are putting countless species at risk.

At ECOS, we believe protecting biodiversity starts with how we live and what we use to clean. By making products with carefully selected ingredients and minimizing our environmental footprint, we help protect the ecosystems that protect us. Because the wonder of the natural world isn’t just something to admire. It’s something to defend..
